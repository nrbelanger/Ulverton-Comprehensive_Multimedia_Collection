Spinning Mule Maintenance
Lubricants

Lubricant Guide

Lubrication is the most common form of preventative maintenance, lubricants are used on gears, pins and bushings, chains, pullies, and rollers. There are many different types of lubricants with different applications and purposes. Early lubricants used on the mill were likely to be seed oils, whale oils and later petroleum derivatives. Grease was also used in situations of higher loads and greater temperatures.
